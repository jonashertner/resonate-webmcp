how

save, organise, share, and protect your atlas.

  1. Keep something.Choose a place, path, or book. Search by name, import a GPX path, or enter a title yourself.
  2. Make it yours.Add a note, choose where it stands, and connect related things with tags.
  3. Create a collection.Gather places, paths, and books for a trip, person, or idea. Nothing is shared yet.
  4. Share when you choose.Review exactly what will leave before creating a link or shared file. Sent copies cannot be recalled.
private by default

Your atlas stays in this browser. There is no account or analytics, and Resonate cannot see it. Map and search services receive only the request needed to answer you.

backups

Download a backup file in Settings. The optional Travellers Club keeps an encrypted copy that only your recovery phrase unlocks. Club backups protect against losing a device, not the service itself. Keep your own backup file too.

Let your assistant plan from recommendations by people you trust.

Resonate is a private atlas for places, paths, and books that remembers who led you there. Its browser tools expose only what you include in sharing. They find and prepare; you decide what to save or share.

No account. Resonate does not receive your atlas or have access to it. It stays on this device unless you share it or use encrypted club backup.

what leaves this browser?

CARTO receives map-tile requests. Photon receives search text after three characters and may receive a coarse map area. Nominatim receives deliberate searches and coordinates when Resonate names a location, photo, map point, or imported path.
